About VLSI Learning Hub

A practical learning resource for Verilog HDL, digital design, synthesis, static timing analysis and VLSI concepts.

Our purpose

VLSI Learning Hub is intended to help learners connect digital design theory with RTL examples, simulation, timing analysis and implementation concepts.

What you can learn

  • Verilog HDL and testbench fundamentals
  • Combinational and sequential digital circuits
  • Synthesis and static timing analysis concepts
  • Low-power design and UPF fundamentals
  • Standard-cell characterization and Liberty modeling concepts

Editorial approach

Technical articles should explain the reasoning, include reproducible examples where possible, and distinguish simplified educational examples from tool- or library-specific behavior. All examples should be checked for correctness before publication.
