Follow a clear path from digital logic and RTL to timing analysis and standard-cell data, with practical examples at every stage.

STAGE 01

Build RTL fundamentals

Begin with Boolean logic, Verilog operators, combinational blocks, registers, counters, and finite-state machines.

STAGE 02

Explore synthesis and timing

Understand constraints, clock definitions, setup and hold checks, slack, and the difference between synthesis and routed timing.

STAGE 03

Learn implementation concepts

Explore standard-cell timing models, Liberty data, low-power intent, and how digital design concepts connect across the flow.
