Standard-Cell Characterization

Explore standard-cell timing, power and leakage characterization, Liberty models, timing arcs, and simulation-based characterization concepts.

1. What characterization produces

Explain how transistor-level simulations and defined test conditions are used to derive timing, transition, input capacitance, internal power and leakage information for library models.

2. Liberty file structure

Introduce library, cell, pin, direction, function, timing, related_pin, timing_type, timing_sense and lookup-table concepts. Clearly distinguish examples from a production library.

3. Timing arcs

Describe input-to-output propagation arcs, setup/hold constraints, recovery/removal checks and transition behavior. Include small diagrams and explain rise/fall conditions.

4. NLDM and CCS concepts

Explain the high-level differences between nonlinear delay models and current-source-based models, and how slew/load conditions affect lookup tables.

5. Testbench and corner setup

Document supply voltage, process corner, temperature, input slew, output load, simulation accuracy and repeatability. Avoid publishing proprietary PDK or foundry data.

6. Debugging characterization

Discuss convergence, unexpected waveforms, measurement thresholds, missing arcs and consistency checks using original, non-confidential examples.

Important: publish only designs, scripts, screenshots and library examples you own or are authorized to share. Do not upload employer or customer proprietary netlists, PDKs, Liberty files or confidential reports.
